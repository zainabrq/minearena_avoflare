# Independent safety and temporal decision policy

**MineArena PS03 | Frozen Architecture V4.1 | Specification v1.0**

Engineering prototype only; clinical decision support, not diagnosis. Implementation, integration and clinical validation pending. All numeric timing and decision heuristics below are configurable engineering defaults, not clinical guidelines. Do not add architecture components or use SNN.

## 1. Execution and precedence
Safety on every authenticated observation before windowing. Routine clinical assessment after each adaptive 5/10/15s window (initial 10s); fixed causal 15s feature frames for PINN-ODE. Current clinical decisions do not wait for model, counterfactual, report or HAPI. Forecasts experimental until validated; may display but cannot independently change priority. Apply order: safety floor -> current clinical evidence -> context/persistence -> eligible forecast evidence -> alert lifecycle. Keep clinical priority, current concern, reliability, monitoring status and active alert separate. Human oversight; no diagnosis or autonomous treatment.

## 2. Explicit prototype rules (not RCP escalation thresholds)
Use 03 component scores, 04 candidate deviations, 05 findings, 06 quality. These are proposed conservative demo policies for validation, not clinically approved triage rules. Evaluate first matching priority from top down; preserve all evidence.

| Rule ID | Trigger | Current priority/action |
|---|---|---|
| SAFE-1 | Fresh reliable HR/RR component 3, SpO2 component 3 due to LOW saturation on applicable scale, or Temp <=35.0 C (component 3) | Urgent; immediate clinical assessment |
| SAFE-2 | Suspect potentially critical raw measurement (including HR/RR/SpO2 zero) with no confirmed artifact explanation | Urgent verification; distinguish uncertain physiology, do not assert diagnosis |
| SAFE-3 | Confirmed artifact-only critical-looking value, no independent reliable critical evidence | Technical verification; insufficient assessment if necessary; retain existing safety floor until authorized resolution |
| CLIN-1 | Two independent reliable domains each score >=2, or persistent moderate concern with corroborating moderate domain | Urgent clinical review in prototype |
| CLIN-2 | Any reliable component score 2, any Scale2 upper-saturation score >=1 on oxygen, or persistent candidate deviation with relevant documented clinical context | Review |
| CLIN-3 | Persistent candidate deviation with independent corroborating vital, or single candidate persistent >=60 eligible seconds regardless of context | Review |
| CLIN-4 | Any score 1, candidate not yet persistent, or concerning but incomplete evidence | Watch with explicit concern/verification; Review if uncertainty requires human assessment; do not claim normal |
| CLIN-5 | All four eligible and no concerning evidence | Watch/continue monitoring |
| CLIN-6 | No applicable higher priority and insufficient data (including missing critical vital) | Insufficient assessment; technical verification as indicated |

Precedence clarification: CLIN-6 overrides routine Watch if current data insufficient to assess; higher safety/Review still applies. Scale2 high-on-oxygen score 3 is not low oxygen; Review unless other rule applies. Raw extreme boundary checks before rounding: HR<=40 or >=131, RR<=8 or >=25, Scale1 SpO2<=91, authorized Scale2<=83, Temp<=35.0. Valid rounded values meeting SAFE-1 also trigger. Zero suspected goes SAFE-2. Confirmed artifact cannot erase pre-existing Urgent; reliable remaining signals can trigger SAFE-1. Safety supervisor works without baseline/context retrieval; use default Scale1 if no authorization verified, explicitly flag unavailable context, request review. Unauthenticated data rejected, technical/security event; never assign unknown data to patient.

## 3. Temporal evidence
Persistence: >=20 eligible abnormal source seconds within trailing 30s and >=80% valid coverage, or continuous >=20s; coverage still required. Cumulative and contiguous duration reported separately. Use actual source timestamps and unique event IDs, not number of packets. Normal observations break contiguous duration but recurrence remains in rolling history. Gaps >2 nominal intervals break continuity; missing never counts as recovery. 60s trend, 5min history; candidate baseline freeze from first concern. Do not wait for persistence for SAFE-1/2 or CLIN-2 component score 2. Adaptive window policy prototype: 5s on suspected abnormality/quality concern, 10s default, 15s after >=60s stable valid monitoring; gateway owns scheduling, no dropped samples; safety bypasses.

## 4. Alert lifecycle
Store independent alert episode ID, identity, category clinical/technical, highest unresolved priority, latest assessment priority, evidence refs, first/last times, acknowledgement, recovery status, resolution actor/reason. States open -> acknowledged -> recovery_verification -> eligible_for_resolution -> resolved; recurrence resets recovery timer and keeps same episode. Escalation immediate (Watch may go direct Urgent). Do not downgrade unresolved Urgent automatically; authorized clinician resolution required after reviewing evidence, with auditable reason. For Review, 60s contiguous valid stable observations, no applicable trigger and no safety floor permits downgrade/closure according to authorized policy; default clinician closure for clinical episodes in prototype. Acknowledgement is not resolution. Missing/suspect data pauses or resets recovery verification (default reset). A normal packet never closes urgent. Technical episodes separately resolve after 10s recovered reliable stream; not clinical closure. Re-notification may occur on escalation or configured reminder, but does not count new episode. New episode only after prior resolution and new trigger. No alerts generated solely by old forecast/state version. Use atomic per-session state version or compare-and-swap; stale async results discarded.

## 5. Outputs, explainability and latency
ClinicalDecisionV1 includes priority, concern, reliability, monitoring status, forecast status, recommended human action, active alert refs, assessment time/state/context/policy versions, each triggering reading/value/unit/time/ref, rule/version, relevant patient fact with evidence ref or explicit unavailable fact, limitations. For generic safety rule without special context explicitly state default scoring and unavailable context; never fabricate fact. Explain raw vs estimated values. Record source sampled time, receipt, safety decision, window close, clinical decision, forecast completion, dashboard publication; provisional targets <2s safety from sampled event, <1s routine after close, <2s forecast after frame, p50/p95/p99 at ten concurrent streams. Report collection/queue latency separately; 10s source batches cannot guarantee <2s safety. Replay source-time for persistence, wall time for performance. HAPI persistence asynchronous durable queue, reconciliation and explicit failures.

## 6. Evaluation contract (mandatory PS03)
Use identical observed runs for fixed comparator and proposed system, independently labelled ground truth. Comparator prototype fixed Scale1 bands, no personalization/quality suppression, Review for any score>=2, Urgent for any score3; missing not zero, raw recorded zero may trigger fixed rule. Same episode counting, resolution policy and replay duration for fair comparison. Freeze comparator and policy before test. Measure first qualifying Review/Urgent after deterioration onset, lead time=severe endpoint time-first qualifying alert, report missed events separately, negative lead time if late. No severe endpoint -> report detection delay from onset separately. False clinical episodes = episodes with no matched labelled deterioration, report artifact-related subset; verification alerts separate category for analysis (report their burden as well). Define event matching intervals from ground truth before test, count one detected event per event, avoid duplicate credit. Alerts/patient-day=clinical episodes/(sum monitored source seconds/86400), report technical and verification separately, include monitoring gaps/exposure convention explicitly. Test stable, slow deterioration, motion/probe, combined deterioration+fault. Report observed metrics only, not examples. Include FHIR validation, evidence completeness and missed detections. No synthetic results as clinical validation.
