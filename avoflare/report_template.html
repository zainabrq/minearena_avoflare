MineArena PS03 · Architecture V4.1 · evaluation

Avoflare post-discharge deterioration monitor

Evidence from 10 simulated patients monitored for 15 days each (1.3 million 10-second decisions). Every model is scored on patients it never trained on. This is an engineering evaluation on synthetic data, not clinical validation.

1 · Event level

Did it catch each deterioration in time?

PS03 contract (07 §6): first Review/Urgent after onset; lead = severe endpoint minus first alert; false episodes = alert episodes not matched to a labelled event. Identical episode rules for every system.

2 · Window level

Accuracy of every decision

Positives are 0.5% of windows, so specificity and PPV carry the false-alarm story; 95% CIs from 1,000 patient-cluster bootstrap resamples.

3 · Continuous risk model

Gradient-boosted risk score (leave-one-patient-out)

Weakest patients are P002 (transient, mild episode) and P006 (episodic). The model can only raise a patient to Review; it never lowers a priority or creates Urgent.

3b · Digital twin

PINN-ODE forecast error on held-out patients

Mean absolute error in physical units on patients the twin never trained on, compared with "no change" persistence and with the mechanistic part alone. Coverage = share of outcomes inside the ensemble ±2 SD band (95% would be well calibrated).

4 · Blood pressure and HRV

Monitoring-only vitals

SBP uses NEWS2 bands; shock index = HR/SBP; HRV score = drop of RMSSD from the patient's baseline (prototype, not a published score). BP baselines are assumptions because the patient records contain no BP history.

Green = built, amber = partial, red = not built. Extra beyond V4.1: care-team escalation agent with phone/watch push, BP and HRV monitoring.

Limits

What this evidence does not show

  • All vitals are simulated from the project's scenario configuration; 9 events across 10 patients is a small sample and CIs are wide.
  • Deterioration labels come from the simulator itself, so this tests the engine against the scenarios it was given, not real-world physiology.
  • Avoflare is slower to first alert than fixed NEWS2 (median 359 s vs 205 s) in exchange for 16× fewer false alerts; fixed NEWS2's earlier alerts partly come from alarming almost continuously.
  • The twin equations are provisional (generic relaxation + the spec's illustrative HR coupling + a learned residual) and need clinical/AI approval; its uncertainty bands are too narrow for RR and SpO2.
  • Clinical use requires validation on real recordings and clinical sign-off of the tuned rules.
